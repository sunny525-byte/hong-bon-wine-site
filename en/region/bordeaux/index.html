Bordeaux

Bordeaux lies in south-west France on the banks of the Gironde estuary, and is among the best-known wine regions in the world. Famous for red blends of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, it also makes dry whites from Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon. Clay, gravel and limestone in different proportions give each plot its own character.
