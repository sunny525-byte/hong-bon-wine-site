WHITE WINE / MINERAL & FLORAL

WHITE WINE / LIGHT & SWEET

Germany — 2024 "STEEP SLOPE" KABINETT

Hand-picked from steep slopes, naturally fermented in stainless steel and stopped by chilling to lock in the natural sweetness, then aged 5-6 months on the fine lees. Delicate aromas of ripe flat peach and elderflower with the classic Mosel slate minerality. Velvet-smooth, balanced between acidity and sweetness, just 8% alcohol — light, refreshing and layered. ✿Awarded 93+ by Falstaff magazine, 2025✿

Region: Mosel, Germany | Grape: Riesling | Alcohol: 8% | Serve at: 8-12°C | Pairing: sorbet, light chiffon cake
