Château de Lagarde (Les 2 Frérots)

Château de Lagarde — Les 2 Frérots, "the two brothers" — sits in Saint-Macaire, one of Bordeaux's oldest terroirs. Fine clay and red gravel define the site; the grapes are picked by hand, giving Bordeaux wines with a clear character of their own.

Region: Bordeaux

WHITE WINE / STONE FRUIT & MINERAL

France — 2021 Lagarde Blanc

From the Saint-Macaire appellation — one of Bordeaux's oldest terroirs — where fine clay and red gravel produce a wine of real character. Aged 6 months in oak, with peach, grapefruit, mineral and creamy nut aromas. Round and silky on entry, elegantly blending fresh citrus with complex aromatics.
