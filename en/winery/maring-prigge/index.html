WINERY · Germany

Maring-Prigge

A new chapter for Germany's oldest wine village.

Two friends, 8,500 km apart, spent years turning a shared idea into reality. Leaving their original careers behind, they found their place on the steep slate slopes of the Mosel, making low-intervention wines that carry a sense of identity.

Riesling is the core, supported by Pinot Blanc and Müller-Thurgau, across two hectares of rare parcels.

The three-colour slate of the Dhroner Hofberg gives intense minerality; the Neumagener Rosengärten blooms with delicate floral and fruit notes.

Everything on these steep slopes is worked by hand, in a spirit of sustainability.

Region: Mosel

RED WINE / WILD & ELEGANT

Germany — 2023 Pinot Noir

Hand-picked from steep slopes, fermented on the skins for about 15 days and aged at least 18 months in small oak barrels. WHITE WINE / LIGHT & SWEET

Germany — 2024 "STEEP SLOPE" KABINETT

Hand-picked from steep slopes, naturally fermented in stainless steel and stopped by chilling to lock in the natural sweetness, then aged 5-6 months on the fine lees.
