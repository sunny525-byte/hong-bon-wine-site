Château Perayne

Château Perayne is a Bordeaux estate built around the idea of "nature and Bordeaux". Its top wine is made only in good vintages and in limited quantity, while the Papillon range centres on Cabernet Franc, aged at length in French oak for a fine, long finish.

Region: Bordeaux

Wines by Château Perayne

01
RED WINE / CLASSIC RESERVE

France — 2020 Papillon Rouge Réserve

Aged 12 months in French oak. Brick-red in the glass with rich, generous aromas of raspberry, truffle and caramel. Full on the palate with jam, coffee and chocolate, lifted by white blossom. Supple tannins and a long finish — complex, open and memorable.

Region: Super Bordeaux | Grapes: Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon | Alcohol: 13.5% | Serve at: 16-18°C | Pairing: pan-seared steak, roast veal, assorted cheeses

02
RED WINE / RARE RESERVE

France — 2020 Papillon Rouge Grande Réserve

Limited to 1,200 bottles and made only in good vintages. From 58-year-old Cabernet Franc vines, aged 30 months in 400 L French oak. Deep gem red with crimson highlights. Intense aromas recalling mocha, plum and black fruit; harmonious and soft on the palate with the minty freshness and sweet pepper typical of Cabernet Franc. The oak adds coffee and caramel, with a long, sweet finish and real ageing potential.

Region: Super Bordeaux | Grape: 100% Cabernet Franc | Alcohol: 14% | Serve at: 16-18°C | Pairing: pan-seared steak, roast veal, assorted cheeses

03
RED WINE / OAK-AGED & VELVETY

France — 2022 Oak-Aged Cuvée Rouge

Aged 12 months in French oak, with barrels rotated each year and different toast levels for added complexity; bottle age rounds it further. Full and round, with ripe red fruit and a light pepper note that lifts meat dishes and chocolate desserts alike.
